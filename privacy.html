Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Puretek Global Solutions LLC, doing business as Puretek Global Cleaning ("Puretek Global Cleaning," "we," "us"), collects, uses and protects information when you visit puretekglobal.net, request a quote, or communicate with us by phone, text or email.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or book a service, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, service address, property details and job notes. We also keep records of our calls, texts and emails with you, and basic website usage data (such as pages visited and device type) collected through cookies and analytics and advertising tools.

How we use your information

We use your information to prepare quotes, schedule and perform cleaning services, send appointment reminders and service updates, process payments, respond to your questions, and improve our services and website.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you provide your mobile number and agree to receive texts, we will send messages related to your quote, scheduling, appointment reminders and service updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us operate our business (such as our scheduling, phone and payment platforms), and only as needed to provide our services to you.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business and are required to protect it, or when required by law.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your choices

You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, unsubscribe from emails using the link provided, or contact us to access, correct or delete your information.
